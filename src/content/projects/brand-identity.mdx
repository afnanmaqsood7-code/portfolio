## Overview

Build One builds homes for clients in the USA, the UK, the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Pakistan. They needed a brand that felt solid and trustworthy, and simple enough to work in every one of those markets.

I designed the full identity: the logo, colour and type system, a 42 page brand book, the complete stationery set, campaign standees and social ads.

## The idea

<Feature src={mark} alt="The Build One monogram B1 in navy and orange." kind="screen" kicker="Concept" title="The 1 is a building">

The name gave me the mark. The final letter of "Build" becomes a bold numeral 1 that rises like a building, in orange, the colour of construction and energy. It reads as a word and a symbol at once, and the 1 works alone on cards, folders and social avatars.

</Feature>

## Stationery

Every printed piece uses the same simple rule: lots of white space, the logo top left, and the navy and orange 1 as a bold signature.

## Campaign

For launch I designed six standees with one playful idea: a home treated as something precious. A house in a pearl, a house wrapped in a scarf, a house floating above the clouds.

<Figure src={standees} alt="Six Build One roll up standees in a gallery: a house from a magic lamp, a floating island home, a house on a tablet, a roof filling a gap, a house in a red scarf and a house in a pearl." caption="Six campaign standees, each one simple idea and one line of copy." />
