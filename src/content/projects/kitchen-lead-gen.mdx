## Overview

A premium UK kitchen brand wanted more enquiries from paid social without looking like a discount brand. Every lead goes to a sales visit, so cheap leads that never buy cost them twice.

I ran the account myself: audience strategy, campaign setup, ad copy, creative, lead form, testing and reporting.

## The problem

  cols={3}

  ]}

## What I did

<Points
  items={[
    { title: '1. Tighter audiences', body: 'I focused targeting on affluent homeowners likely to renovate.' },
    { title: '2. Qualifying lead form', body: 'Short questions on budget, timeline and project type filtered out people who were not ready.' },
    { title: '3. Craft, not discounts', body: 'New creative and copy sold quality and craftsmanship instead of price.' },
    { title: '4. Test, keep, repeat', body: 'Each round kept what worked and replaced what did not, with a lower daily budget.' },
  ]}
/>
