## Overview

A SaaS platform in Florida came to me with a Google Ads account that was spending well and earning badly. Leads were expensive, and many of them were not real buyers.

I ran the account myself: strategy, campaign setup, ad copy, creative, testing, tracking and monthly reporting.

## The problem

<Points
  cols={3}
  items={[
    { title: 'Tracking counted the wrong things', body: 'Any form fill counted as a win, so Google optimised for cheap, low quality signups.' },
    { title: 'Broad traffic', body: 'Broad match keywords pulled in searches with no buying intent.' },
    { title: 'A leaky landing page', body: 'Visitors who did want a demo had to work too hard to book one.' },
  ]}
/>

## What I did

<Points
  items={[
    { title: '1. Fixed tracking first', body: 'I rebuilt conversions around qualified demos, trial signups and real form submissions, so the algorithm learned what a good lead looks like.' },
    { title: '2. Split keywords by intent', body: 'I moved away from broad match into tight phrase and exact match groups, each with its own ads and budget.' },
    { title: '3. Rebuilt the landing page', body: 'One clear offer, fewer form fields and proof near the button, so ready buyers could book quickly.' },
    { title: '4. Tested and reported monthly', body: 'Ad copy and page tests ran every month, with a simple report on cost per lead and lead quality.' },
  ]}
/>
