## Overview

Axivus makes car repair paperless and easy to trust. One platform connects the customer, the technician, the service advisor at the front desk and the shop owner, so a repair moves from check in to payment without paper forms or chasing calls.

I was the only designer. I reworked the mechanic app, designed a new iPad app for service advisors and a new web command centre for shop owners, and built one design system for all three. We shipped V1 in two months.

## The problem

Repair shops run on calls, texts, notebooks and paper repair orders. That breaks down in three places.

  cols={3}

**The goal:** one clear flow for every repair, where each person sees only what they need and the customer can approve every step.

## The rule behind the product

Everything follows one line: **technicians find, advisors decide, customers approve.**

Working on three apps at once showed there are two kinds of job. On a **shop job**, the advisor owns the estimate and talks to the customer, and the technician only inspects and repairs. On an **independent job**, the mechanic does it all. Writing this down settled who sends the estimate, who the customer messages and how they pay.

## Mechanic app

Mechanics use the app standing up, with busy hands, between jobs. Every screen answers one question: what's happening, and what do I do next?

<Feature src={dashboard} alt="Home screen with the current job, items that need attention, today's route and nearby jobs." kicker="Home" title="What can't wait comes first">

The job in progress sits at the top, then approvals waiting, payments to collect and customer replies, then today's route. The next action is always one tap away.

</Feature>

<Feature reverse src={market} alt="Marketplace cards with payout range, distance and trust badges." kicker="Marketplace" title="Bids built on trust">

Each job card shows payout, distance and badges for a verified customer, OBD codes and photos, so a mechanic can decide in seconds. Filters for Today and High payout match how they really pick work.

</Feature>

<Feature src={axiTouch} alt="Axi Touch summary with before and after examples for grouping, wording and related work." kicker="Estimates" title="Honest quotes, with an assistant">

The builder shows parts, labour, margin and take home as the mechanic types. Before sending, Axi Touch groups line items, rewrites jargon in plain words and suggests related work, with a before and after view so the mechanic stays in control.

</Feature>

<Feature reverse src={guest} alt="Guest approval screen: approve by link with no account needed." kicker="Approval" title="No account needed">

Customers approve an estimate from a secure link, with no sign up and no app. This removed the step where repairs most often stalled.

</Feature>

<Feature src={receipt} alt="Receipt with the escrow breakdown and two staged releases." kicker="Payments" title="Escrow protects both sides">

The customer funds the job up front, and the mechanic sees it marked funded before starting. Money is released in two stages: when the repair starts and when the customer signs off.

</Feature>

## Reflection

<Points
  items={[
    { title: 'Design the edges early', body: 'Scope changes and objections are where real jobs fail. Designing them first made the product believable.' },
    { title: 'Trust is a feature', body: 'Escrow, guest approval and a visible history did more for the experience than any single screen.' },
    { title: 'Write the rules down', body: 'The shop job or independent job rule saved more time than any component.' },
    { title: 'Next: test on the floor', body: 'Watch mechanics use it with gloves, noise and interruptions, and measure where jobs stall.' },
