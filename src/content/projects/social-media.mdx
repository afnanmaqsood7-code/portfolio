## Overview

A good post has about one second to work. I designed social media for clients across food, health, education, finance and international trade: single posts, carousels and full campaigns, always built so the feed looks like one brand.

## Campaigns

<FeedPhone brand="confidence" handle="brandbeat" kicker="Brand campaign" title="Instill Confidence">

A four post series for an agency. Each child's shadow shows the person they could become, a dancer, a footballer, an explorer. One idea, repeated, so the series is instantly recognisable.

</FeedPhone>

<FeedPhone brand="africa" handle="uae.africa" kicker="Trade event" title="Counting down to AfCFTA">

A countdown campaign for the African Continental Free Trade Area, with a bold number each day, the map of Africa and speakers announced as the date came closer.

</FeedPhone>

## Education and finance

<FeedPhone brand="edufox" handle="edufox" kicker="Education" title="Study abroad, made simple">
