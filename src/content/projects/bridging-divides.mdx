## Overview

The Forum is the internal reporting system for **Bridging Divides**, a research program at Toronto Metropolitan University. It tracks outputs for more than 60 researchers and 10 staff, and feeds the accountability reports the program sends to its funders.

I redesigned its report template so research outputs are easier to record, track and report — and so the work that never fit the old form finally counts.

</Split>

## The problem

The old template was built for one kind of output: papers and conferences. Everything else fell through the cracks.

  cols={2}
  items={[

### What the audit showed

Reading real project reports line by line made the gaps obvious. A single report could mention a published paper, a community workshop, a new data tool and a partnership — but only the paper had a place in the template.

</Split>

## Key decisions

<Cards
  cols={2}
  items={[
    { kicker: 'Decision', title: 'Complement, don’t replace', body: 'The team debated replacing the old system. We chose to complement the existing Manage Outputs section, so staff could adopt it without disruption.' },
    { kicker: 'Decision', title: 'Design for staff first', body: 'Researchers were too busy to report, so staff became the main users. The template is built around their workflow, with bulk entry and auto-fill.' },
    { kicker: 'Decision', title: 'A home for research tools', body: 'A new Research Tools & Infrastructure module captures dashboards, data portals and protocols instead of hiding them under publications.' },
    { kicker: 'Decision', title: 'Qualitative and quantitative together', body: 'Participant counts, equity tags and progress statuses sit alongside traditional outputs, so impact is described, not just counted.' },
  ]}
/>

Reporting moved from a manual, inconsistent process to a structured one. Staff spend less time duplicating entries, reports need fewer corrections, and the program can finally show the full breadth of its impact — including the community, equity and technical work that used to be invisible.
