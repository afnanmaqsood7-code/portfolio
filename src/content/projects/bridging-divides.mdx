## Overview

The Forum is the internal reporting system for **Bridging Divides**, a research program at Toronto Metropolitan University. It tracks outputs for more than 60 researchers and 10 staff, and feeds the accountability reports the program sends to its funders.

I redesigned its report template so research outputs are easier to record, track and report, so the work that never fit the old form finally counts.

</Split>

## The problem

The old template was built for one kind of output: papers and conferences. Everything else fell through the cracks.

## My role

I worked as the UX designer and strategist for four months, alongside one developer and the ten staff who manage outputs for more than 60 researchers.

- Audited the Forum categories against the funder's reporting categories
- Sketched and wireframed the new template
- Designed a structure that fits what funders ask for
- Documented the process so it can be automated later

## Research process

1. **Audit.** I compared the Forum's categories with the funder's reporting categories.
2. **Gap analysis.** I marked which outputs were missing or filed in the wrong place.
3. **Staff feedback.** Informal sessions with staff showed how they really track outputs.
4. **Sketching and wireframes.** I explored the form on paper first, then in wireframes.
5. **Alignment.** The team reviewed the new categories to make sure they met funder rules.

## Results

Reporting moved from a manual, inconsistent process to a structured one. Staff spend less time duplicating entries, reports need fewer corrections, and the program can finally show the full breadth of its impact, including the community, equity and technical work that used to be invisible.

<Quote text="Balancing qualitative and quantitative data let the template capture the true breadth of research impact." />
