## Overview

Two very different packaging projects. One is warm, natural and handmade. The other is precise, premium and built around strict legal rules. Both needed a system: one look that stretches across many products without losing what makes each one different.

## Blends of Nature

A natural skincare brand from Kenya, made with Africa's raw materials. I designed packaging for the whole range: body oil, beard oil, body butter and coffee scrub.

<Feature src={jars} alt="Cream jars with the Blends of Nature monogram on kraft brown, one jar open showing the lid." kind="screen" kicker="Idea" title="Africa, without the clichés">

Every product carries a real African pattern, leopard, zebra or cowhide, paired with a calm monogram and lots of space. The prints make each product easy to tell apart on a shelf, and the shared monogram keeps them one family.

</Feature>

## What I learned

<Points
  items={[
    { title: 'Systems beat single designs', body: 'A clear rule for colour and pattern made adding new products easy.' },

    { title: 'Material is part of the message', body: 'Kraft, glass and gold foil say as much as the graphics.' },
