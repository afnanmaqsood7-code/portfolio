## Overview

In an emergency, buildings still rely on alarms, radios and people shouting instructions. Evacovation puts the whole response in one app. The chief warden declares what is happening, floor wardens get clear instructions, staff confirm where they are, and everyone can see progress until the building is safe.

I designed the app's flows and screens, and the campaign for its launch.

## The challenge

People use this app at the worst possible moment: stressed, rushed and sometimes in smoke or noise. Every screen had to be understood in a glance and every action had to be hard to get wrong.

## What I learned

<Points
  items={[
    { title: 'Design for stress', body: 'Large targets, one action per screen and strong colour meaning.' },
    { title: 'Confirm what matters', body: 'Only actions that affect other people ask for confirmation.' },
    { title: 'Roles shape screens', body: 'The same emergency looks very different to a warden and to staff.' },
