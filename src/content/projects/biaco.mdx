## Overview

Biaaco Med is an online healthcare partner for families in Pakistan. In one app, a patient can talk to a healthcare facilitator, see a doctor on video, order medicine, book lab tests and manage insurance.

I designed the app end to end: the flows, all 64 screens and the visual system.

## The challenge

Many of Biaaco's patients are older or new to smartphones. They trust a phone call more than a form. The app had to feel as easy as calling a family doctor, while still offering everything online for people who want it.

## A consultation

<Feature src={home} alt="Home screen with a large Connect Online button, a message field and shortcuts to call a doctor, order medicine and book lab tests." kicker="Home" title="One big button">

The home screen leads with a single round button: Connect Online. Below it sit the three things patients do most, call a doctor, order medicine and book a lab test, plus a bar for voice or text messages.

</Feature>

<Feature src={queue} alt="Waiting screen with an estimated wait of two minutes and a library of healthcare videos." kicker="Waiting" title="Waiting that feels useful">

While a facilitator or doctor joins, patients see an honest countdown and can watch short health videos about their symptoms, or hang up and ask for a call back.

</Feature>

## What I learned

<Points
  items={[
    { title: 'Design for the least confident user', body: 'If it works for a grandparent, it works for everyone.' },
    { title: 'Humans are a feature', body: 'Offering a call at every step built more trust than any screen.' },
    { title: 'Never block care', body: 'Optional steps that can wait should always be skippable.' },
    { title: 'Next time', body: 'I would test the flows with real patients early and add Urdu throughout.' },
  ]}
