## Overview

Rabia Welfare Hospital is a family hospital in Lahore, serving patients since 1981. They needed a business profile to share with partners, donors and patients, and a social presence that felt as caring as their staff.

I designed the 21 page profile, its chapter openers and the hospital's social media posts.
